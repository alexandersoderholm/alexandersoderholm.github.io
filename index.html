This is me

Alexander Söderholm Master of Architecture Student Based in Lund, Sweden

Currently in my fifth year of architecture school, I find myself constantly drawn to design that creates personal encounters. For me, that means shaping spaces where people actually want to engage, alongside practical choices that make a project feasible. When those big-picture ideas merge with the small details into a clear story, that is good design to me.
