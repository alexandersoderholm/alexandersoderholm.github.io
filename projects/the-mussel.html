The Mussel

2026 Academic Seaside Pavillion
Team Jesús Manuel Martín García, Maria Alejandra Casas Ruiz & Zahra Khoshooei
Location Helsingborg, Sweden
Focus Public addition to existing mussel farming
My role Visualizations, building massing, & platform geometry
Documents Posters (PDF)

This project explores how a public marine platform can integrate with existing aquaculture in Helsingborg, combining education, food culture, and environmental awareness to reconnect the city with the sea.

Located in Helsingborg, the proposal complements existing mussel farming by creating a visible landmark that brings attention to marine cultivation and the ecological potential of the sea, building on earlier initiatives such as Havoteket.

The building functions as an educational and experimental space where visitors can explore marine life through workshops, observation areas, and laboratory-like spaces equipped with microscopes. An accessible roof provides an additional public space for gathering, relaxing, and enjoying the harbor environment.

Encouraging social interaction through activities like cooking mussels and public workshops throughout the year, the project seeks to strengthen the connection between the city, its inhabitants, and the surrounding marine ecosystem.
