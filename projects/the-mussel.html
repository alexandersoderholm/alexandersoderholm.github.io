The Mussel

Adding a public platform to the existing mussel farming.

The proposal integrates a public marine platform with existing aquaculture, combining education, food culture, and research to reconnect the city with the sea. Located by the harbour, the building complements mussel farming through observation spaces, public workshops, and an accessible rooftop gathering space.
