Depth of Field

2026 Competition Winner Museum Extension
Team Sara Holmgren & Julie Wójcik
Location Södermalm Stockholm, Sweden
Focus Bridging the professional nature of the musem to the publicness of the metro
My role Design, spatial layout, overground connection, diagrams, photographic storytelling & branding
Documents Competition Results (Archiol) Posters (PDF)

Depth of Field re-evaluates the relationship between infrastructure, culture and the urban fabric in Södermalm, extending Fotografiska underground to connect the newly constructed Sofia Station Blue Line metro, the waterfront and the street level.

By connecting underground transit flows with cultural programming, the intervention relieves the historic above-ground customs building from restrictive window coverings, allowing heritage architecture to host appropriate programs while high-capacity visitor flows, cinemas and analogue workshops unfold in fluid, excavated spaces below.

The spatial sequence features key architectural elements including The Lens (a cinema typology linked directly to the metro transit) and The Chamber (cylindrical workshop volumes formed around an abandoned train tunnel). Together, they establish a continuous, memorable architectural journey through lighting, oculus shafts and graphic branding patterns.

Juror’s Comment

An exceptional and highly resolved proposal that masterfully integrates infrastructure, city, and culture. The concept of extending Fotografiska underground to connect metro, waterfront, and urban fabric is both visionary and pragmatic. The spatial sequence from metro descent to the “lens” and cylindrical exhibition space, creates a powerful and memorable architectural journey, while the integration of cinema, workshops, and public programs establishes a vibrant cultural hub.
